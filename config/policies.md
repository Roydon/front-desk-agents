## P1 Check-in and check-out
Slips: check-in from 2:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am. Campsites: check-in from 3:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am.
Arriving after the office closes (6:00 pm)? Your welcome packet, site or slip map and gate code will be
in the after-hours box by the office door, labelled with your name. The dockmaster's evening line is
(555) 010-2200 until 9:00 pm.

## P2 Rates
Transient slips: $3.50 per foot of boat length per night, 30-foot minimum. 30/50-amp shore power and water included.
Seasonal slips (May 1 – October 15): $95.00 per foot for the season.
RV sites, full hookup (water, sewer, 50-amp; RVs up to 40 ft): $68.00 per night.
RV sites, water + electric (30-amp; RVs up to 32 ft): $52.00 per night.
Tent sites (up to 6 people, 2 tents): $35.00 per night.
Rates do not include state and local taxes.

## P3 Deposits and payment
A deposit of the first night is due at booking; the balance is due at check-in. Seasonal slips: 25% at
signing, balance by May 1. Payments are taken through our secure payment link or in person. We never
take card numbers by email, text or voicemail.

## P4 Cancellations and changes
Cancel 14 or more days before arrival: full refund minus a $25.00 fee. 7–13 days: 50% refund. Fewer than
7 days: no refund. Holiday weekends (Memorial Day, July 4th, Labour Day): 30 days' notice required for any
refund. Date changes are free if made 14 or more days before the original arrival, subject to availability.

## P5 Holiday minimum stays
Memorial Day, July 4th and Labour Day weekends have a 3-night minimum for slips and campsites.

## P6 Insurance
Every vessel staying at the marina must carry liability insurance of at least $300,000 naming Lakeside Cove
Marina as additional insured. Please send your certificate before arrival.

## P7 Vessel registration
A copy of current state registration or USCG documentation is required before arrival.

## P8 Rental agreement
Every reservation needs a signed rental agreement before arrival. We send it by email with your confirmation.

## P9 Pets
Up to 2 pets per slip or site. Pets must be leashed outside your boat, RV or tent, and never left unattended.

## P10 Quiet hours
10:00 pm to 7:00 am.

## P11 Fuel dock and pump-out
Fuel dock (gas and diesel): 8:00 am – 6:00 pm daily, May through October; 9:00 am – 5:00 pm November through April.
Pump-out is free for marina and campground guests during fuel-dock hours.

## P12 Boatyard services
Haul-out and block: $14.00 per foot. Winterization and storage are quoted by the boatyard. Service requests
are scheduled by the boatyard team, usually within 3 business days.

## P13 Launch ramp
Day launch: $15.00 per day. Free for registered marina and campground guests.

## P14 Trailer and vehicle parking
Boat trailers park in Lot B: $10.00 per day, free for guests with a slip reservation of 3 nights or more.
One vehicle per campsite; extra vehicles park in Lot A.
